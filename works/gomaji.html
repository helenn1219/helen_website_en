Challenge

This was GOMAJI's first large-scale physical carnival under a brand-new identity. The site had to do two things at once: introduce a company-wide rebrand to a member base built over a decade, and turn that attention into an actual purchase-and-redemption flow. With only 3 weeks for planning and design combined, the challenge was making a single-page site clearly communicate both "the brand is new" and "this event is worth showing up for" — without either message drowning out the other.

Approach

I designed for three target groups — families, fan-community audiences, and existing app/e-commerce users — carrying the new brand color system and the new mascot character through a single-page scrolling layout that segmented event highlights, featured food vendors, and logistics info, with the ticket-purchase and coupon-redemption entry point kept visible at every scroll position on both desktop and mobile. I worked directly with marketing and the vendor-partnership team throughout to make sure the on-site redemption flow matched what the site promised.

Outcome

The site hit both goals: on the transaction side, it drew 5,000+ attendees and 42 partner vendor booths on-site, with an 80% coupon redemption rate; on the brand side, it reached over 6 million member impressions, making it one of the highest-visibility moments in GOMAJI's rebrand year.
